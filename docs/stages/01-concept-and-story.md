# Stage 1: Concept and story

**Date:** before 2026-10-21

## Goal
Decide what kind of game this is before writing any code.

## Decisions
- **Platform:** Android, published on the Play Store. Also runs on desktop for development.
- **Offline only.** No servers to pay for, so everything (world, saves, story) runs on the phone.
- **2D, top-down 3/4 view, pixel art.** It's a real game built in an engine, not a web page.
- **Combat feel:** inspired by the real-time dodge-and-strike combat of *Pokemon Arena 2D*
  (Kid Nation). We use the mechanics only, with no Pokémon names, characters or art.
- **Open world without loading screens.** The world streams in around the player as he walks.
- **Built as a prototype first,** so the team can be recruited with something playable.

## The story so far
- Two kingdoms, **A** and **B**, are at war. **Kingdom B wins.**
- **Kael**, 19, is from Kingdom A. He lives alone, with no family, no friends and no real
  bond to his homeland.
- After the war he slips into Kingdom B's capital, **Akropolis Aigidos**, and is taken in
  by **Haldor**, an old blacksmith who becomes a father figure.
- **Part 1 has no magic or powers.** Kael is an ordinary young man.
- **The royal family of Kingdom B:** the king and queen, their two sons and one daughter,
  and the king's sister with her son and daughter.

## Mission 1
Haldor sends Kael outside the walls to mine iron ore. Kael forges a sword with Haldor, then
hunts down the leader of a beast pack (the Terrak) to bring back moonsteel.
